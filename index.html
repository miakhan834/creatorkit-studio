03 / BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Does CreatorKit create finished videos?

CreatorKit creates editable video plans: scripts, visual prompts, voiceover text, and subtitle files. It does not render video or generate recorded voiceovers. Take the exported plan into your production tools to finish your video.

Where are my projects saved?

Projects are saved locally in your browser, without an app account. They do not sync across devices and can be lost if you clear site data. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy.

What should I check before publishing?

Review AI-generated scripts for accuracy, refine the prompts for your visual tools, and check subtitle timing against your final audio. The plan is a starting point you can edit.
